Privacy Policy

Mafia: The Underground Society is a portfolio project: a multiplayer social deduction game. This page explains what it stores about you.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and display name. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email and profile picture from Google. We never receive your Google password.
  • Password: if you sign up with email, your password is stored only as a secure hash.
  • Game progress: games played, wins and experience points, used for your level, rank and the leaderboard.
  • Sessions: a session record (including IP address and browser user agent) so you stay signed in.

In-game chat messages are kept in server memory only for the duration of a game and are never written to the database.

How it is used

Only to run the game: signing you in, showing your name, avatar and level to the players in your room, and ranking players on the leaderboard. Your data is not sold, shared for advertising, or used for any other purpose.

Where it is stored

Account and progress data is stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted by Neon. The game server runs on Render and the website on Vercel.

Deleting your data

You can permanently delete your account and all associated progress at any time from Your Dossier → Delete account inside the game.
